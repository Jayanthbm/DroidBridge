To control your phone with your Mac keyboard & mouse (without Accessibility or ADB):

  1. Click Open Bluetooth Settings below.
  2. Make your Android phone discoverable in its Bluetooth settings.
  3. Select your phone in macOS Bluetooth Settings and click Connect.
  4. Confirm the pairing prompt on both devices. Once bonded, it reconnects automatically.
